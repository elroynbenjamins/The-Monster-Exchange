# Privacy Policy for The Monster Exchange

**Effective date:** August 28, 2026  
**Application ID:** `com.elroybenjamins.themonsterexchange`

The Monster Exchange is developed and published by Elroy Benjamins ("we", "us", or "our"). This policy explains how the current release of The Monster Exchange handles information.

## Summary

The current release is an offline, local-save game. It does not require an account and does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. Game progress and preferences remain on the player's device unless the player independently copies or backs up that data using device or platform features.

## Information handled by the app

The app creates local gameplay information such as save progress, monster collections, settings, and a player-chosen display name. This information is used only to operate the game on the device. The display name does not need to be a real name.

The current release does not include:

- user accounts or developer-operated cloud saves;
- advertising or advertising identifiers;
- analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting services;
- location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera collection;
- in-app payments or collection of financial information; or
- developer-operated servers that receive gameplay or personal data.

## Storage, retention, and deletion

Game data is stored locally on the player's device and remains there until the player clears the app's storage or uninstalls the app. Because we do not receive this data, we cannot retrieve or delete it remotely. Players can delete all app data through the device's application settings or by uninstalling The Monster Exchange.

The app currently has no account system, so there is no separate account-deletion process.

Device manufacturers, operating-system providers, app stores, and user-selected backup services may process information under their own privacy policies. For example, an operating system may include app data in a device backup if the player enables that feature. We do not control those independent services.

## Data sharing, sales, and advertising

We do not sell personal data. We do not share personal data with third parties, and the current release contains no third-party advertising or behavioral tracking SDKs.

## Security

The app minimizes privacy risk by keeping its gameplay data on the device. No storage method is guaranteed to be completely secure, so players should protect access to their device and any backups they create.

## Children

The current release does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other players. If a future release introduces online features or data collection, this policy and the app's age-audience declarations will be reviewed before release.

## International users

Because the current app does not transmit personal data to us, we do not transfer player data between countries. Platform-provided distribution or backup services may operate internationally under their own terms.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date above will be revised, and material changes will be published with or before the relevant app update. Features such as accounts, online multiplayer, cloud saves, analytics, advertising, payments, or developer-operated crash reporting must not be released until this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration are updated.
